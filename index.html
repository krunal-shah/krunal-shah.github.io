Current interests

Long-horizon reinforcement learning

Post-training for agents

Agent systems and evaluation

I work on post-training and agent systems at Yutori. I’m especially excited about long-horizon reinforcement learning: how agents can learn from delayed outcomes, recover from mistakes, and remain coherent across long tasks.

At Yutori, I contribute to the training and systems behind Navigator, Scouts, and Delegate. My work spans on-policy expert distillation, data and evaluation, and the orchestration infrastructure needed to run tool-using agents reliably.

Previously, I worked on Gemini model quality and helped launch Gemini Canvas. Before that, I worked on applied perception for Google Assistant. I studied computer science at the University of Pennsylvania and electrical engineering at IIT Delhi.
